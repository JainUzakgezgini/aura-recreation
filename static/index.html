Real Mode Bonus

Use Aura on any real website. Runs 100% in your own browser: no install, no data sent.

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: press Ctrl + Shift + B (Mac: Cmd + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar: Aura Real Mode
  3. Open any website (for example Wikipedia) and click the bookmark. The Aura panel opens in the bottom-right corner.
  4. In the panel: pick a Profile, turn on Enforcer to really scan the page and fix low contrast text, tiny buttons and images without alt text, and turn on Clarify for easier reading.
  5. Click the bookmark again (or ×) to close Aura. The page goes back to normal.
