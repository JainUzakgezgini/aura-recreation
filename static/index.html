Real Mode Bonus

Use Aura on any real website. Runs 100% in your own browser: nothing is sent anywhere.

The Aura panel opens in the bottom-right corner of the page. In the panel you can:

  • Profile: change the colors (Voyager, Beacon or Guardian).
  • Enforcer: really scan the page and fix low contrast text, tiny buttons and images without alt text.
  • Clarify: easier reading with bigger text and more spacing.
  • Narrative: read the page, or the text you selected, out loud with your computer's own offline voices.

Option 1: Bookmarklet

No installation, no permissions, works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Good for a shared computer.

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B (Mac: Cmd + Shift + B).
  2. Drag this button to the bookmarks bar: Aura Real Mode
  3. On any website, click the bookmark. Click again to close.

Option 2: Chrome extension

For daily use: Aura sits in the toolbar and opens with a keyboard shortcut.

  1. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder of this project.
  3. On any website, click the Aura icon or press Alt + Shift + A.
